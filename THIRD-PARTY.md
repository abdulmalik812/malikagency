# Assets and dependencies

The hero/story 3D scene renders the Marble Bust 01 scan (CC0, see below) as dark chrome metal with a custom scroll-driven shard-assembly shader in `components/experience/scroll-sculpture.tsx`. Three.js, RoomEnvironment and the GLTF loader do the rendering.

The earlier unused laptop/bust files and old sculpture component are omitted from the full source archive. They may remain in an older folder after applying the additive changed-files patch, but are not loaded.

Original agency logos and founder image are retained. Open-source packages retain their own licenses in their installed packages. The locally bundled Inter font license is included in `public/fonts/`.

The loading-screen tiles in `public/loader/` are technology logos from the devicon project (https://devicons.github.io/devicon/, MIT license), plus an original screenshot tile of the client's LOLPDF project.
